Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. Your plan remains active until the end of the billing period.

What happens to my images if I downgrade?

Your generated images remain accessible. If you exceed the free plan's history limit, older images may be removed after 30 days.

Do unused generations roll over?

No, unused generations reset at the start of each billing cycle. They do not carry over to the next month.

Is there a free trial for Pro?

Yes! New users get a 7-day free trial of the Pro plan. No credit card required to start.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers for Enterprise plans.

Do I own the images I generate?

Yes. All images you generate are yours to use commercially or personally, subject to our Terms of Service.
